Strategy Notes

  • Judge a player on their fixture run over the next 3–6 Gameweeks, not one big haul or one blank.
  • Avoid a -4 point transfer hit unless it's for an injury or suspension — it rarely pays off otherwise.
  • Wait for team news close to the Friday/Saturday deadline before transferring, unless chasing a price change.
  • Only back a low-ownership differential if their underlying stats and fixtures actually support it.
  • Prioritize nailed-on starters over higher-ceiling players who are rotation risks.
  • Save your Wildcard for a genuine squad overhaul, not a few bad picks.

2026/27 chip rules: 8 chips total — one Wildcard, Free Hit, Bench Boost, and Triple Captain per half of the season. The first set must be used before the Gameweek 19 deadline and does not carry over.

How This Works

What the terms and colors on this page actually mean.

Fixture Difficulty Rating (FDR)

FPL's own 1–5 rating of how tough a fixture looks, from that team's point of view. 1–2 (green) is an easier fixture, 3 (gray) is average, 4–5 (coral) is a tough one. It's a blunt tool — a good defense on paper can still concede, and vice versa — so treat it as a starting point, not gospel.

xGI and xGI per 90

Expected Goal Involvements — a stat that estimates how many goals and assists a player "should" have produced based on the quality of chances they were involved in, regardless of whether they actually went in. "Per 90" adjusts for minutes played, so a player who's only started a few games can still be compared fairly against a nailed-on starter.

Nailed-on starter vs rotation risk

Based on how many of the team's finished games a player has actually started this season. A high ratio means they're rarely rested or benched — a more reliable source of points than a talented player who might not even play.

Differential

A low-ownership player (under 10% here) who could gain you rank if they perform, since few other managers have them. This dashboard only calls out a differential when their underlying stats (xGI per 90) actually support it — being unpopular alone isn't a reason to pick someone.

Clean sheet likelihood

Blends a team's own recent defensive form (goals conceded per game, last 5) with their next opponent's recent attacking form (goals scored per game, last 5), with a small adjustment for home advantage. "Good chance," "Toss-up," and "Unlikely" are the three verdicts you'll see.

Set-piece takers

Pulled from each player's penalty, free-kick, and corner order (1 = primary taker). This is usually more reliable early in the season than FPL's own set-piece notes, which can take a while to get populated.

Get / Monitor / Avoid and Keep / Monitor / Sell

A heuristic score combining fixture run, nailed-minutes reliability, underlying stats, set-piece involvement, and injury/rotation flags. "Get" or "Keep" means the signals line up well right now — not a guarantee of returns. Use it as one input among several, not the final word.

League table filters (All / Home / Away)

"Home" and "Away" recompute the whole table using only each team's home or away results — useful for spotting teams that are strong at home but shaky on the road, or vice versa.
